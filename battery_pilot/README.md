# Battery Pilot

Plans and steers a Solis home battery against your contract's prices, the solar
forecast (corrected for tree shade it learns per month and hour) and the capacity
tariff, and compares fixed with dynamic contracts on your own measured energy.

It starts in shadow mode. Once you switch on "Battery Pilot control", it sets the
Solis time-of-use slot, charge and discharge currents and the grid-charge switch,
always for the current hour only. It has its own page in the sidebar.

See the Documentation tab for the full guide: every setting, what it reads and what it changes.
