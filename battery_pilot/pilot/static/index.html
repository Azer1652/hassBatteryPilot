How to read this

The chart has four panels on one time axis: the battery level, two strips with what the plan wanted (grid charge, discharge, self-use) and what the inverter did, and the solar and house energy per hour. In every panel a solid line or bars is what was measured and a dashed line what the plan expected. Tap or hover over the chart for the values at each moment.

Day ahead is the plan made just after midnight, right after the nightly learning, from the solar forecast after shade and your usual consumption. Its errors add up over the day. The plan in force at the start of each hour is kept too: it starts again from the real battery level, so its error is one hour's worth ("off at the end of each hour" below, and the hourly table). Days are kept for 60 days.

Fixed or dynamic? Your contract comparison

"Same usage" prices what you actually bought and sold on both contracts. "Best battery use" replays each day with the battery strategy that fits each contract (cheap-hour charging, export, surplus solar, the winter night target), including battery wear, and blocks export at negative sell prices when that switch is on. Contract fees and the capacity tariff are included.
